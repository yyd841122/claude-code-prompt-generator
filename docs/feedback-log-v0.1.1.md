## 1. Feedback Collection Goal

Collect first-round real feedback on v0.1.1 from real users and acquaintances. Key observation points:

- Can the site load correctly?
- Can users understand what this tool does?
- Are there obvious mobile issues?
- Is the homepage copy too technical?
- Is anyone interested in AI solo founder / AI tool development process?
- Does anyone suggest feature requests?

## 5. What Counts as Useful Feedback

- Someone cannot open the page
- Someone doesn't understand what the tool does
- Someone thinks there is too much English
- Someone thinks the page is too long
- Someone finds the buttons hard to locate
- Someone asks how this was built
- Someone asks if it can be turned into a different prompt tool
- Someone suggests adding a specific template

## 6. What Not to Overreact To

- One person saying it doesn't look good does not warrant an immediate redesign
- Non-target users who don't understand AI at all does not mean the product has no value
- No comments on WeChat Moments does not mean failure
- No immediate user acquisition does not mean the direction is wrong
- Do not add complex features based on a single piece of feedback

## 7. Review Plan

Review at 24 hours and 72 hours after posting.

Each review:

- Count total feedback entries
- Categorize issues
- Pick 1-3 most worthwhile issues to fix
- Do not fix too many at once
